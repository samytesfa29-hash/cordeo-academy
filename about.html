THE COR DEO PARTNERSHIP

Your child’s education.
Our shared calling.

An education works best when the people who care about a child are working together.

Cor Deo’s university-model approach combines on-campus instruction with parent-guided days at home. Students spend three days with their teachers and peers and two days following teacher-prepared assignments with their families.

Teachers choose the curriculum and plan the lessons. Parents guide the follow-through. The result is a connected week of learning, grounded in a Christian worldview.

WHAT GUIDES US

Faith is our foundation.

Christian discipleship connects what students learn
with who they are becoming.

Our mission

We partner with parents to nurture Christian disciples through Scripture, everyday application, and traditional academics taught from a Christian worldview.

Our vision

We seek to prepare graduates whose faith and character shape how they serve their families, churches, workplaces, and the wider world for Jesus Christ.
