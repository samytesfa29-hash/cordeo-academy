# A short walkthrough for the principal

Start with: "I focused on making Cor Deo's identity clear and helping families understand the school, find practical information, and take the next step."

1. Show the homepage: the photographic first impression, school/family message, and tuition information near the top.
2. Select a photograph thumbnail, then choose A glimpse inside. The nine-photo story opens at the selected hero image. Show Next, Previous, and Escape.
3. Try the weekly schedule: Tuesday is a teacher-planned home day; Wednesday is back on campus.
4. Change the grade in the home-day guide. The estimate and parent involvement update. Explain that the time is student work time and the sample sequence is illustrative.
5. Open Admissions to show the enrollment steps, local official packet, published tuition, and family estimate.
6. Open School Life to show nine different photographs, the existing school video link, and the upcoming/past calendar filters.
7. Open Our Approach to show school office contacts and the beliefs link. The Faith page links to the complete official statement.
8. Explain contact honestly: families can prepare an email or use the school's existing form during preview. The new site does not send inquiries or book appointments automatically.

Ask for feedback on the design, photo choices, information accuracy, and preferred inquiry process. Show the GitHub Pages preview first; moving the school's domain is a separate launch step.
