ACCESSIBILITY

A welcoming site.
For every visitor.

The website is designed to support keyboard navigation, readable content, and a range of screen sizes.

Ways to navigate

Use the “Skip to content” link at the top of each page. Keyboard focus is visible throughout the site. The mobile menu closes with Escape. In the weekly schedule, use the left and right arrow keys to move between days; Home and End go to the first and last day.

Reading and motion

Text can be enlarged with your browser’s zoom controls. Layouts adapt to smaller screens. Images have descriptive alternatives, and the site respects your device’s reduced-motion preference.

Need help?

If a feature is difficult to use or you need information in another format, contact the school at info@cordeoacademy.org or 503-442-8053. This page describes design features, rather than making a claim of formal accessibility certification.
