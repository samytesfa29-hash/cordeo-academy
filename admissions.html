YOUR PATH TO COR DEO

Four steps.
A personal welcome.

  1. 01

    Come meet us.

    Tour campus with Principal Robert Arndt. Ask questions, see the school, and talk about your child.

    Request your tour
  2. 02

    Get to know the partnership.

    Read the enrollment packet, including the family commitments, statement of faith, tuition schedule, and school policies.

  3. 03

    Complete your application.

    Prepare one family application and a separate student application for each child. New families also provide the additional records and assessment requested by the school.

  4. 04

    Return your forms and deposit.

    The school lists a $400 non-refundable family deposit, applied toward tuition. Return the required paperwork to the office and confirm placement directly with the school.

BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Let’s answer
your questions.

Which grades can apply?+

The school advertises enrollment for kindergarten through 10th grade. Contact the office about current openings, grade placement, and the classes available for your child.

How do we get started?+

Start with a campus tour with Principal Robert Arndt. Then review the enrollment packet and complete the required family and student paperwork. See the admissions steps.

Can we visit before applying?+

Absolutely. A tour is the first step. You can see campus, meet the principal, and talk through how the partnership would work for your family. Request a tour.
