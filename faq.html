Ask us a question
What is a hybrid school?+

It combines classroom instruction with parent-guided learning at home. Cor Deo students attend campus on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday. On Tuesday and Thursday, they complete teacher-planned work with their parents.

Do I need to choose curriculum or write lesson plans?+

No. Cor Deo provides the curriculum, lesson plans, and home-day assignments. Parents guide the work at home and stay in touch with teachers. Some grading is shared, depending on the grade and subject.

Which grades can apply?+

We offer kindergarten through 10th grade. Contact the office about current openings, grade placement, and the classes available for your child.

How involved will I be on home days?+

Younger students need hands-on guidance. Older students work with increasing independence. Your role includes helping your child stay organized, understanding the assignments, and following through. See the home-day guide.

Is Christian faith part of everyday learning?+

Yes. Bible study, Christian character, and a Christian worldview are central to the school’s educational purpose. Families review the school’s statement of faith and commitments as part of enrollment.

Where is the school?+

Cor Deo is at 4545 NW Kaiser Rd, Portland, OR 97229, in the Bethany area. Families come from Beaverton, Hillsboro, Cedar Mill, Tigard, and across the Portland area.

How do we get started?+

Start with a campus tour with Principal Robert Arndt. Then review the enrollment packet and complete the required family and student paperwork. See the admissions steps.

How much is tuition?+

The school’s published 2026–27 rates are $6,200 for K–6 and $6,325 for grades 7–8. Ask the office about grades 9–10. Published sibling discounts are 10% for a second child and 20% for additional children. The enrollment packet and school office are the authority for fees, eligibility, and payment terms.

Can we visit before applying?+

Absolutely. A tour is the first step. You can see campus, meet the principal, and talk through how the partnership would work for your family. Request a tour.

What comes after 10th grade?+

Talk with Principal Robert Arndt about planning for grades 11–12 before deciding on your child’s next step. Ask about current course offerings, academic records, and transition planning. We can discuss your family’s situation during a campus visit.

Where can we read the statement of faith?+

Start with our faith and beliefs page, then read the complete school statement in the enrollment packet. The principal can discuss questions about the school’s Christian worldview and family commitments.
