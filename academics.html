KINDERGARTEN THROUGH 10TH GRADE

Learning is a journey.
We walk it together.

Meet the school to discuss specific curriculum,
current classes, and the right placement for your child.

01

KINDERGARTEN–2ND

The early years.

Early learning calls for close partnership. Teachers introduce new work in class, and parents guide home-day practice with patient encouragement.

  • Hands-on parent involvement at home
  • Teacher-prepared lessons and assignments
  • A Christian foundation for school life
02

3RD–6TH GRADE

Growing foundations.

As students become more capable, the partnership grows with them. Parents help keep work organized, explain when needed, and review progress.

  • A connected rhythm of class and home work
  • Practice taking responsibility for assignments
  • Continued guidance from teachers and parents
03

7TH–10TH GRADE

The next chapter.

Older students take increasing ownership of their schoolwork, while parents encourage good habits and help them stay on track.

  • Growing independence on home days
  • Teacher instruction on three campus days
  • Grade placement discussed with the school

Ask the office about current 9th–10th grade offerings, course availability, and tuition.
